How the mark forms

The loop is the train: its roof, its rounded nose and its floor. The bowl is the tunnel’s arch, turned 90° to face it. The brand calls the mark Echo, because a name goes into the tunnel and its address comes back.

bar 12 · beat 1
  • Traced from the real GIFThe lines follow the cartoon’s actual outlines, pulled from the frame where the nose meets the tunnel.
  • Snapped to the brand’s gridThen they become the exact mark: 6 units thick, round ends, even gaps of 7.21 and 5.77.
  • Also Dai’s ĐWith its upright taken away and its two lines joined into a loop. That is the optional beat in shot 09.

Why it is built this way

The lore builds to the drop, and the drop is the brand

Believers know every face and meme in the montage, so it plays as it does in the film, wordless, for 22 seconds. Its last flash before the drop is the empty tunnel. When the music lands, the train arrives, and the film turns from grainy footage into the clean navy and gradient of the product.

After the mark forms, each bar carries one idea: the name, how to pay, that it is yours, where to get it.

Before I render

What I need from you

  1. The track file

    The repo has the song only inside S0_Somebody.mp4. Up to the drop that mix is music only, so the intro can come from it, but after the drop the music sits under Heart’s voice. I need the MP3 or WAV you bought, the one the film’s build reads as S0_MUSIC. It was bought for the film, so please check the licence also covers a promo for pDAI Names.

  2. Swap one montage beat

    Bar 9, beat 1 is a third-party post that names a person as the exploiter, with his photo. The film shows it under a “Claim” stamp. In an ad it would have no stamp and would read as the ad accusing him. I recommend the swap, to the pDAI Conference art (shot 02).

  3. The strobe in bar 11

    As boarded, S0’s strobe on Heart’s diamond video flickers with the empty tunnel and lands on it, so the drop has something to answer. The other option is to keep S0’s strobe untouched.

  4. The Đ beat in shot 09

    It shows the mark’s second origin, Dai’s D with its upright taken away. It costs no extra time. Keep it or drop it.

  5. Credit the montage

    A small “From The pDAI Mystery” tag on the montage marks it as an excerpt from the film. It keeps the people in it from reading as endorsing pDAI Names. I recommend it if the film is public or about to be.

  6. The link on the end card

    The README still lists the domain under “Not done yet”. Please confirm pdai.domains is live before I render shot 14.

  7. The example names

    yourname.pdai in shots 10 and 11, believer.pdai in 12 and 13. Any name you prefer works.

Production

How it gets made

  • The montage is re-rendered with S0’s own ext_montage.py, with the one swap and the new bar 11, so everything else matches the film frame for frame.
  • Shots 04–14 are HTML and SVG scenes rendered frame by frame at 30 fps with Playwright, the way S0 was built. The mark is drawn from the same path the site and the on-chain name cards use.
  • Sound is the track plus a few synthesized effects (whoosh, click, ping, ticks), mixed to about −16 LUFS like the film.
  • The master is 1920 × 1080 H.264 with AAC. Vertical and square cuts can follow once this one is approved.
