## Delivery

- **Voice:** the narrator of *The pDAI Mystery*, with its settings, so the montage arrives with its own voice.
- **Lines 1–4:** low and close, at documentary pace. Line 4 is nearly a whisper, since the music almost stops there.
- **Lines 5–11:** brighter and quicker, one idea per line, each landing just after its cut.

## Recording and the mix

- One file per line is easiest, since each line goes on its own cue. One take with about two seconds between lines also
  works. MP3 or WAV.
- The voiceover sits at −16 LUFS, like the film's narration. The music ducks about 6 dB under each line and comes back
  for the drop and the logo.
- Line 11 gets the music's half-beat echo, so the name echoes out with the last hit.
- If a take runs past its "Out by" time, the cut can move by a beat around it.
